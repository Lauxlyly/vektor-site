What this is — and what it is NOT

  • IS: a real, pre-registered, out-of-sample falsification of this one mechanical ruleset.
  • IS NOT a claim about any specific person, channel, or named methodology.
  • IS NOT a claim that "breakout strategies lose" — it is one rule family, one universe, one window.
  • IS NOT a claim about your strategy. Your rules, your discretion, and your risk management may differ — that's exactly what a Falsification Review checks.
  • IS NOT a prediction. Out-of-sample past performance does not bind the future.
  • It is a proxy of stated rules, not of anyone's live discretion. A generous proxy can still miss a real discretionary edge — and if the edge lives entirely in discretion, that means it is unfalsifiable as stated, which is itself a finding.
